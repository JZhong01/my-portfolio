When SVB collapsed in March 2023, USDC broke its dollar peg while USDT traded at a premium as participants fled to perceived safety. Using 150,000+ minute-level order book snapshots across Kraken, Binance, and Coinbase, this paper traces how that confidence shock propagated through Bitcoin spot market microstructure. We construct a cross-currency basis framework documenting a 1,452 bps peak USDC dislocation, arbitrage half-lives expanding from minutes to 12+ hours, and a novel order book signature — one-sided bid depth accumulation in BTC/USDC — revealing that traders used Bitcoin as an escape vehicle to rotate into USDT, a pattern distinct from the bilateral withdrawal seen in equity crises. Results replicate across all three exchanges, confirming market-wide reserve-quality repricing. This work was recognized as an award winner in the IAQF Academic Affiliate Member Paper Competition.
