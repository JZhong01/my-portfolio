In this project, I coded the following ensemble learning techniques — Boosting (AdaBoost and Gradient Boosting), Bagging, and Random Forests — front scratch and analyzed how they improve predictive accuracy beyond individual classifiers. I compared ensemble methods against baseline models, analyzed feature importances, and evaluated the effect of ensemble size and depth on bias-variance trade-offs.
