In this project, I implemented (from scratch using only NumPy, Pandas, and basic SciPy functionality) and compared three supervised learning classifiers — Decision Trees, Logistic Regression, and Support Vector Machines — to evaluate their performance across classification tasks. I explored hyperparameter tuning, decision boundaries, and model evaluation metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and ROC-AUC, gaining hands-on experience with the trade-offs between interpretability and predictive power across different model families.
