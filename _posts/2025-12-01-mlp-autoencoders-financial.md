In this project, I coded (fully from scratch) and trained Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) networks and Autoencoders on financial time series data. I implemented feedforward networks for classification and regression tasks, and explored autoencoders for dimensionality reduction and anomaly detection, gaining experience with neural network architecture design, backpropagation, and optimization.
